// What it does

Nine intelligence modules.

Every aspect of a Facebook profile or post — extracted, scored, and visualized.

01

Profile collection

Posts, photos, reels, about data, comments, and full commentor metadata.

02

Comment intelligence

Per-comment sentiment, emotion, stance, and language in 5+ languages.

03

Actor scoring

Weighted composite score. 5-tier classification from Strong Supporter to Critical Voice.

04

Country detection

LLM-based country identification from city, hometown, employer, education, and language.

05

Face intelligence

HOG-model face detection with 128D encoding and identity clustering.

06

Network graphs

Force-directed graphs, co-interactor matrices, and stance-based hierarchies.

07

Image analysis

OCR plus AI vision — scene, objects, political symbols, location clues.

08

Threat reports

Professional PDF reports with charts, network visualizations, and summaries.

09

Fully Local

Everything runs locally via Ollama. Your investigation data never leaves your machine.
